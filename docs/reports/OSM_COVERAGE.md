Same ODbL licence, so no extra licence is needed. Nodes use their point; ways use the mean of their nodes; relations use a point inside the polygon.

- 38 POIs lay just outside every district polygon (coast/border, < ~2 km) and were given the nearest district; 2 were further away and are not counted.
- "Named" = has `name`, `name:bn` or `name:en`. "Phone" = `phone`, `contact:phone`, `phone:mobile`, `contact:mobile` or `mobile`. An object tagged both amenity and shop is counted once, under amenity.
